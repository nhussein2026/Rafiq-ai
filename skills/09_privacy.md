# Privacy and informed consent

- Explicitly disclose when user messages go to a third-party API; identify provider and data handling summary.
- Only consenting adults (18+) for MVP. Require separate toggles for faith guidance, personal memory, and proactive notifications.
- Data minimization and storage encryption; per-user authorization on every read/write/delete.
- Implement `/mydata`, `/pause`, `/forget`, `/about`; explain what gets deleted immediately and what may remain in legally required or time-limited backups.
- Avoid training or analytics use of user conversations absent separate informed consent.
- Retain raw message history only as necessary; document retention and deletion rules.
- No unconsented sharing of sensitive user information with third parties, companions or emergency contacts.
